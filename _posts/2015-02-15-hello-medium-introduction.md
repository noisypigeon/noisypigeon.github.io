I have decided to start writing weekly articles. You may have read my first publication about [why I dropped out of high school](/posts/why-i-dropped-out-of-high-school/). I am writing today to describe what my readers are in for and why you should come along for the ride. I left school in pursuit of a life filled with happiness, working with startups and like-minded individuals. I love entrepreneurship, travelling, hiking and languages.

I want to bring the optimists along for a life filled with adventure, fulfilment and pleasure through my words and video blogs on my YouTube channel.

I am writing these articles because I am on a mission to positively impact other people’s lives, inspiring them to do something that wasn’t thought to be possible. Our time on Earth is limited, so let’s not waste it. I want to bring my readers with me through my experiences around the world.

> “People have a problem with me being different, but that propels me forward in life.” — Mary-Louise Parker

These articles are being written by me, an individual who does not hide himself, or hold back. I would rather regret doing something than regret not having done it. Life brings billions of opportunities that will change indefinitely. I am looking to bring insight to my readers and will precisely break down my ideals, dreams and ambitions over time, allow you to comprehend what the lifestyle I am working towards costs: physically, psychologically, financially, everything.

I am going to be writing about entrepreneurship, startups, culture, software engineering, psychology and most importantly, the experiences I have. At roughly an article a week, I will start to grow a library of thought-provoking material that is digestible by anyone. Let’s go where nobody will go, be the people nobody thought we could be and experience things that will mould us into the people we want to be.
