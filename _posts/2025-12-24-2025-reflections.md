2025 was a year I identified patterns that no longer serve me and embodied that I could stop surviving and choose to start living. I worked relentlessly to put down what was never mine to carry and instead put intention towards learning what I want. It was far from graceful but at least on my own terms.

* * *

## 26,000 Kilometers

-   Got my Class 7N driver's license, bought my first car, and drove 26,000 km around southern BC. Road trips, random lakes, learning the roads like the back of my hand.
    
-   Reclaimed visiting cities and towns I grew up in. Going back on my own terms.
    
-   Buying clothes and equipment I want and keeping them. Breaking the pattern of feeling obligated to sell them if I don't use them "enough."
    
-   Gleam is turning 3 at the end of December. Celebrating her growth alongside my own, working on her reactivity from prior attacks, building her confidence. She's healing too.
    
-   Replaced my Nissan Kick’s cabin air filter for $16 instead of $150 at a service centre, despite my mobility limitations getting under the dash.
    
-   **455 days in my current home; the longest in years. Building a home, not just “a place to stay,” believing it is not something I’ll catastrophically lose.**
    

* * *

## What Was Owed

-   Worked through the Montreal backlog: my condo sold finalizing the foreclosure, resolved a Quebec rental dispute, and finished loose ends for my dissolved corporations.
    
-   Consolidated fragmented credit bureau files with Equifax and TransUnion through a combined 22 formal disputes.
    
-   Reorganized remaining debt, identified additional fraud, and worked with third parties to write off accounts that were obfuscated from me.
    
-   Built a comprehensive financial tracking system: iterating on a spending framework that works with my brain/accessibility needs.
    
-   Completed my last consumer proposal payment. Awaiting the certificate of performance in the new year.
    
-   **Paid ~$54K in 2025 and ~$83K total across 2024-2025 towards debt left in my name.**
    

* * *

## The Work

-   Identified agency as the central organizing principle of my recovery work.
    
-   Built a personal crisis toolkit: regulated tools, intentional friction, safety planning, decision frameworks. Systems that work with my brain instead of against it.
    
-   Faced estranged family members after 11+ years. Not for reconnection, but for my own closure. Grieving a relationship that will never come back. Starting to put down the weight carried for that time.
    
-   Found my footing at Wealthsimple. Identifying my strengths, leaning into them, growing into the role while staying true to how I work best. Approaching 2 years in April, the longest I've worked anywhere.
    
-   Living as Willow Finch. The first name I’ve fully independently chosen, returning to Willow after almost a decade.
    
-   **Advocating for myself in ways that were not safe in the past: with healthcare providers, at work, in relationships.**
    

* * *

## For Its Own Sake

-   First practical sewing project completed: repaired a hole in a sweater using my Singer 411G.
    
-   Buying many books I want to read but haven't gotten to yet (using my professional development budget from Wealthsimple).
    
-   Invested in technical growth: sharpening Rust and Go skills, using LLMs as a disciplined force multiplier in daily work and side projects.
    
-   **Discovered certification as a hobby. Earned: Class 7N (car license), fishing license. Exploring: Watercraft, Class 1 (semi-truck), Class 6 (motorcycle), PAL, and amateur radio operator.** 
    

* * *

## Back in My Body

-   Connected with a supportive primary care clinician. Referrals to chronic pain clinic and neurology in progress.
    
-   Overcame hospitalization due to respiratory complications due to a Covid infection. Survived, recovered, kept going.
    
-   Rebuilding my relationship with medication. Able to take pills again, more consistent with my prescribed routine.
    
-   Rebuilt functional strength through a gym routine, learning that rest is part of the process. From "couldn't carry a coffee cup" to hitting PRs: chest press up to 35lbs, low row up to 45lbs.
    
-   Taking steps toward what I want: consistent laser sessions every 1.5 months, starting the long waitlist process for GRS.
    
-   **Started the process of getting a wheelchair as a mobility aid. Breaking the pattern of reinjury with structural support that can do the work on bad days and augment my capacity on good days.**
    

* * *

## Integration

-   Consolidated all cloud storage platforms into Backblaze. Single source of truth instead of scattered across services.
    
-   Relaunched my blog. Public presence rebuilt on my own terms, folding in fragments from across the years, multiple names and aliases.
    
-   **Built a system to reorganize my 8TB of files: identifying duplicates, tracking revisions to contextualize documents across years of accumulated data.**
    

* * *

## Intentions for 2026

-   Getting my wheelchair. Gaining access to the chronic pain clinic and neurologist. Structural support to break the cycle of reinjury.
    
-   Moving through the Montreal era. Consumer proposal discharged, remaining debt cleared. Legally becoming Willow Finch.
    
-   Visiting Berlin. An extended working holiday in a place I once felt seen, safe, and had agency. Returning to feel it again, not just remember it.
    
-   **Getting a manual car. GR Corolla is the goal, with room for a Civic Si, GR 86, or WRX as the bridge. Reclaiming what was gatekept and denied. Not just a preference, but agency and engagement. Perfecting heel-toe downshifts. Something embodied. Something mine.**
