I use this computer for my full-time job as a Support Engineer (read software engineer who helps other software engineers) where I answer tickets, edit and compile projects, and work with virtual machines. After hours I use it to play Stardew Valley, Forza Horizon 4 and CS:GO at 1440p 60, as well as 3D CAD using Fusion 360 and music production using FL Studio.

## Parts

-   **CPU**: AMD Ryzen 5 2600 3.4 GHz 6-Core.
    
-   **CPU Cooler**: Noctua NH-U12S SE-AM4.
    
-   **Motherboard**: MSI B450M Mortar Titanium Micro ATX AM4.
    
-   **Memory**: G.Skill Trident Z 16GB (2×8GB) DDR4-3200 CL14.
    
-   **Storage**: Samsung 970 Evo 1TB M.2-2280 PCIe 3.0 X4 NVMe SSD.
    
-   **Video Card**: MSI GeForce GTX 1070 TI Titanium 8GB.
    
-   **Case**: Fractal Design Meshify C Mini MicroATX Mini Tower.
    
-   **Power Supply**: EVGA SuperNova G3 750W 80+ Gold Fully Modular ATX.
    
-   **Operating System**: Windows 10 Pro OEM Key.
    
-   **Case Fans**: 5x Be Quiet! Silent Wings 3 50.5 CFM 120mm.
    
-   **UPS**: CyberPower CP1500PFCLCD.
    
-   **Custom**: USB 3.0 20-Pin Internal Header Adapter Cable (Low Profile).
