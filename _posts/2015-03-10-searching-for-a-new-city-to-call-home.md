I established that I really dislike staying in the same place for long, building up daily routines or doing “normal day-to-day activities”. I have never been one to “go with the flow”, as I can never hold my tongue when I see an inferior solution being enforced. I have interests in technology, architecture, psychology and culture, but the problem that I face is every little thing that was once unfamiliar, is now known to me like the back of my hand. I have an inner passion for exploring, seeing foreign things and getting lost in new places.

At the end of the summer in 2014, I realized that Vancouver was starting to lose its charm. I am no longer exhilarated by the culture, people and vibe that western Canada provides. I set myself on a journey to find a new city, somewhere else around the world that provides me with the satisfaction I am looking for. I have been curating and refining a list of destinations that I am going to visit before my eighteenth birthday in pursuit of finding a new city to call home.

My list of destinations are as follows:

-   Montreal, Canada
    
-   Bordeaux, France
    
-   Berlin, Germany
    
-   Prague, Czech Republic
    
-   Oslo, Norway
    
-   San Francisco, United States
    
-   London, England
    
-   Vienna, Austria
    
-   Istanbul, Turkey
    
-   Stockholm, Sweden
    
-   New York, United States
    
-   Cape Town, South Africa
    
-   Zadar, Croatia
    
-   Toronto, Canada
    

As would be expected, there are an infinite number of other destinations that I have interest in visiting, but from my own research, these fourteen destinations could potentially house my future. Since the summer, I have visited San Francisco (in October) and New York (in December). ~~You can watch my daily video blogs on my YouTube channel~~ and with just over two months in between travels, it’s time to go again.

My next destination is going to be Montreal. I have found that Quebec holds a unique Canadian culture, having various interesting districts, people and a foreign language. I am planning a trip that consists of flying to the city from YVR and staying in a Airbnb for a couple weeks to experience the city while I work and explore.

Maybe I’ll find that place that makes my _clock tick clearer_, that’s the main objective of these travels. To find somewhere I’d like to call home.
